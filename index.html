02

ABOUT

A HARDIN ICON,
REBORN

The building on Center Avenue hasn’t changed. The hands running it have. Owner after owner, handoff after handoff, this place has been taken apart and put back together, and each time it came back a little better than it was.

We’re the latest set of hands, and we know the work isn’t finished. We’re doing our best: fixing what’s broken, keeping what works, and raising the standard a little at a time. One roof, four rooms: a dining room, a bar, a casino, and a working movie theater, all of it here for Hardin.
